# Smart PDF Study Assistant

**Salahaddin University – Erbil | College of Science | Computer Science & IT Department**  
**Team:** Mahdi Luqman Abdulrahman (Team Leader), Dler Karzan Mahmood

## What Is This Project?
Smart PDF Study Assistant is a simple website built to help students study smarter for exams. A student uploads a study **PDF file** (class notes, lecture slides, or textbook chapters), and **Gemini AI** turns it into:
1. A **Short Summary**
2. **Real Exam-Style Quizzes** (Multiple Choice, True/False, Fill-in-the-Blank, Short Q&A, and Answer in Your Own Words)
3. **Interactive Flashcards**
4. **Customization** & **Export / Import** for saving and reusing study sets

## Development Plan
- **Step 1:** PDF Upload & Summary Logic
- **Step 2:** Quiz: Multiple Choice & True/False
- **Step 3:** Blanks, Short Q&A & Own Words
- **Step 4:** Interactive Flashcards
- **Step 5:** Add Customization
- **Step 6:** Add Export & Import Logic
- **Step 7:** Integration, Testing & Design
